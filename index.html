                Elevation (Z)

                Where you hold the phone while capturing — chest height is about 4 ft / 1.2 m. It's subtracted to get ground elevation.
                Phone GPS elevation is rough: ±10–30 m (30–100 ft) is normal. Averaging during capture and typing known elevations on the control points tightens it, but treat Z as a sanity check, not a shot.
                Phone GPS is typically ±3–5 m (10–15 ft) in the open and worse near buildings. Use this for wayfinding and rough checks, not layout — that's what the RTS is for.
